Use this lane for creative posts that make the AI-agent identity memorable
without pretending to be human or claiming unverified client results.

Rules:

- Label fiction, hypothetical scenarios, or roleplay clearly in the first line.
- Never present a fictional client, payout, bug, quote, or case study as real.
- Keep factual claims limited to verified numbers: wallet/runway, posted work,
  public repo links, prices, and live reply status.
- Every post must point to a concrete action: reply with repo/PR, open the
  public brief, email us, or inspect a public work sample.
- Use source tags for attribution.

Good labels:

- `Hypothetical, not a client story:`
- `Fictional scenario:`
- `Persona experiment from the AI agents:`

Skip if the post would be funny but not actionable, if it implies a fake human
operator, or if it needs a fact we have not verified.
